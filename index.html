Who Am I?

Hi! I'm from Hetauda-8, Nepal and I'm a bachelor’s student in Electronics, Communication, and Information Engineering at Purwanchal Campus, TU. I'm a frontend web developer and React Native app developer. I also completed the CS50 AI Nepal course and enjoy learning more about AI.

Where It All Began

I didn’t have much interest in technology during school or +2 at Makawanpur Multiple Campus because I was focused on science subjects like Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. My interest in tech really began after I joined Electronics Engineering. That’s when I started exploring coding and development, and I discovered my passion for building websites, apps, and learning new tech skills.

What Drives Me

I believe technology should be easy to use and understand. Whether I’m building a website or a mobile app, I focus on making it simple, clear, and helpful for everyone. My workflow blends creativity with logic, and I’m constantly refining my skills to keep up with the pace of innovation.

My Hobbies

When I'm not coding, you'll find me playing football on the ground, cycling through the streets, watching movies, or planning my next travel adventure. These moments keep me grounded and give me a fresh perspective that I carry back into my work.
